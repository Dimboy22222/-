## Google Search Console (do this first)

1. Go to search.google.com/search-console and add a **URL prefix** property: `https://dimboy22222.github.io/-/`
2. Choose the **HTML tag** verification method and copy the tag. It goes into the `<head>` of `site/index.html`. Run `npm run pages` afterwards so the other pages get it too.
3. Once verified, open **Sitemaps** and submit `sitemap.xml`.

Google then knows about all 6 pages. Search traffic usually takes weeks to months to build.
